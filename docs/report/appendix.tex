\section{Data Record Structure and Core Fields}
\label{app:data-record-structure}

The corpus and evaluation pipeline use line-delimited JSON records so that individual documents, provisions, chunks, questions, predictions, and runs can be inspected and joined without loading the complete dataset. Table~\ref{tab:data-record-structure} summarizes the core identifiers and representative fields required to reproduce these joins; it should be interpreted as a record inventory rather than a formal JSON Schema specification.
\begin{table*}[t]
\centering
\scriptsize
\caption{Core records used by the corpus and evaluation pipeline.}
\label{tab:data-record-structure}

\begin{tabular}{p{0.14\textwidth}p{0.22\textwidth}p{0.16\textwidth}p{0.40\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Record} & \textbf{Required identifiers} & \textbf{Structure} & \textbf{Representative fields} \\
\midrule
Document
& \texttt{doc\_id}
& JSON object
& title, document type, issuing authority, issue/effective dates, validity status, raw-source provenance. \\

Provision
& \texttt{provision\_id}, \texttt{doc\_id}
& JSON object
& heading, article/clause/item path, sequence, parent provision. \\

Chunk
& \texttt{chunk\_id}, \texttt{provision\_id}, \texttt{doc\_id}
& JSON object
& passage text, sequence, embedded-text variant, reading-order pointers. \\

Question
& \texttt{qa\_id}
& JSON object
& question, category, answer type, reference answer, ground-truth document/provision/chunk ID arrays. \\

Prediction
& \texttt{qa\_id}, \texttt{run\_id}
& JSON object
& generated answer, ordered context IDs, citation diagnostics, refusal marker, status, and error. \\

Run manifest
& \texttt{run\_id}
& JSON object
& model/checkpoint, corpus snapshot, benchmark/configuration identifiers, seed, code snapshot, and timing artifacts where recorded. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table*}

Identifiers are stable across pipeline stages. A chunk resolves to one provision and document, while prediction records join to benchmark questions through \texttt{qa\_id} and to experimental conditions through \texttt{run\_id}. Ground-truth evidence is represented by document, provision, and chunk identifier arrays rather than by a text span alone.

For unanswerable questions, the gold evidence arrays are empty by construction. Prediction records retain failed or skipped statuses in the evaluation ledger, whereas answer-overlap metrics use only valid successful answerable rows. These conventions define the joins and evaluation denominators used in Sections~\ref{sec:implementation-setup} and~\ref{sec:ablation-study}.

Appendix~\ref{app:example-objects} provides compact examples of the corresponding document, provision, chunk, and graph objects.

\section{Example Documents, Provisions, Chunks, and Edges}
\label{app:example-objects}

The following compact example shows how one source is represented without
including a full legal document:

{\scriptsize
\begin{verbatim}
document: doc_id=LAW-EXAMPLE-2024
          type=law, effective_date=2024-01-01
provision: provision_id=LAW-EXAMPLE-2024-A15
           article=15, parent=LAW-EXAMPLE-2024
chunk: chunk_id=LAW-EXAMPLE-2024-A15-02
       sequence=2, text="..."
edges: DOCUMENT_HAS_PROVISION -> A15
       PROVISION_HAS_CHUNK -> A15-02
       CHUNK_NEXT: A15-01 -> A15-02 -> A15-03
\end{verbatim}
}

The visible passage can be shortened for a prompt, but its parent identifiers
remain unchanged. A relation to an unavailable instrument is represented as
an external stub rather than a fabricated document:

{\scriptsize
\begin{verbatim}
external_stub: stub_id=EXT-8841
               reference="Instrument X, Article 4"
relation: LAW-EXAMPLE-2024 --CITES--> EXT-8841
status: unresolved_target=true
        retrievable_text=false
\end{verbatim}
}

These examples make the graph and retrieval joins explicit. They also explain
why a high-ranked document can be counted as a document-level success while a
specific article or chunk remains absent from the retrieved evidence.

\section{Graph Relationship Vocabulary}
\label{app:graph-vocabulary}

Graph edges are directed and retain a source record, target record, relation
type, provenance, and (when available) effective-date or confidence fields.
The structural and legal relations used by L\_RAG are summarized in
\hyperref[tab:appendix-edge-vocabulary]{Table~\ref*{tab:appendix-edge-vocabulary}}.

\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\scriptsize
\caption{Graph relation conventions.}
\label{tab:appendix-edge-vocabulary}
\begin{tabular}{p{0.40\columnwidth}p{0.45\columnwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Edge} & \textbf{Direction and use} \\
\midrule
\texttt{DOCUMENT\_HAS\_PROVISION} & Document to contained provision. \\
\texttt{PROVISION\_HAS\_CHUNK} & Provision to indexed chunk. \\
\texttt{CHUNK\_NEXT} & Earlier chunk to next chunk in reading order. \\
\texttt{PROVISION\_NEXT} & Earlier provision to next provision in reading order. \\
\texttt{CITES} & Citing instrument to cited instrument. \\
\texttt{AMENDS}/\texttt{REPEALS} & Later instrument to the instrument it changes or terminates. \\
\texttt{REPLACES} & Superseding instrument to the replaced instrument. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Only verified directions are exposed to query-time traversal. An uncertain
relation can remain in the audit ledger or point to an \texttt{ExternalStub},
but it is not silently promoted to an authoritative retrievable edge. The
validity overlay also records unknown status explicitly; it does not infer
current law from a similar title.

\section{Prompt and Output Formats}
\label{app:prompt-output-formats}

The Base and CoT conditions use the same retrieved context and output budget
in the primary GPT-4o-mini comparison. The retrieval/graph family instead sets
no explicit client-side output-token cap and uses the Vietnamese fallback phrase
\textit{``Không có đủ thông tin trong ngữ cảnh được cung cấp.''}. The Base prompt asks for a concise
answer supported by the supplied passages and provision-level citations. The
CoT prompt adds an explicit reasoning instruction, but the saved artifacts
retain only the final answer and observable diagnostics, not hidden reasoning.

The output contract is conceptually:
{\scriptsize
\begin{verbatim}
answer_type: boolean | extractive |
             abstractive | unanswerable
answer: final answer text or INSUFFICIENT_CONTEXT
citations: [{doc_id, provision_id,
             chunk_id, marker}]
\end{verbatim}
}

The parser records citation presence, the number of unique sources, structural
coverage of scored factual sentences, invalid markers, and refusal markers.
These fields measure formatting and parser behavior. They do not determine
whether a cited provision entails the adjacent statement, whether an answer
is legally complete, or whether abstention was substantively appropriate.
