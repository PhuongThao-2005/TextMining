\section{Dataset and Benchmark}
\label{sec:dataset-benchmark}

This section describes the evidence base behind the experiments: a processed
Vietnamese legal corpus, a graph that retains its structure and relationships,
and a 500-question benchmark with explicit evidence provenance.

\subsection{Corpus Description}
\label{sec:corpus}

The corpus contains Vietnamese legal instruments, including National Assembly
Laws, Government Decrees, Prime Minister Decisions, and Ministry Circulars.
The ingestion pipeline preserves document metadata, hierarchical provisions,
and retrieval passages rather than treating each source as unstructured text.
Table~\ref{tab:corpus-stats} summarizes the resulting scale.

\begin{table*}[t]
\centering
\small
\caption{Processed Vietnamese legal corpus.}
\label{tab:corpus-stats}
\begin{tabular}{llr}
\toprule
\textbf{Entity Type} & \textbf{Description} & \textbf{Total Count} \\
\midrule
\textbf{Documents} & Full legal instruments (Laws, Decrees, etc.) & 151,624 \\
\textbf{Provisions} & Hierarchical units (Articles, Clauses, Items) & 1,386,267 \\
\textbf{Chunks} & Fixed/structural retrieval passages & 1,513,376 \\
\textbf{Document Edges} & Amend, replace, cite, and repeal relationships & 883,256 \\
\textbf{External Stubs} & Referenced external entities & 19,763 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table*}

Before indexing, each passage receives the metadata needed to reconnect it to
its legal source:
\begin{equation}
    \begin{aligned}
        \text{Chunk\_Meta} = \{&\text{chunk\_id}, \text{doc\_id},
        \text{provision\_id},\\
        &\text{title}, \text{issuing\_authority},
        \text{effective\_date}\}.
    \end{aligned}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Knowledge-Graph Structure (G-LRAG Schema)}
\label{sec:kg-schema}

The G-LRAG graph makes statutory structure available at query time. It has
three node types: documents ($D$), provisions ($P$), and indexed chunks ($C$).
Documents retain identifiers, authority rank, issuing body, effective date,
and validity status; provisions retain their header and type; chunks retain
their text and sequence number.

Two families of directed edges encode how evidence should be interpreted:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Structural edges} connect a document to its provisions, a
    provision to its chunks, adjacent chunks in reading order
    (\texttt{CHUNK\_NEXT}), and adjacent provisions
    (\texttt{PROVISION\_NEXT}). They support recovery of nearby statutory
    context.
    \item \textbf{Legal-relation edges} record amendments, replacements, and
    citations between legal instruments. They support validity-aware filtering
    and relationship-aware retrieval.
\end{itemize}

The graph is deliberately conservative. Draft or unverified records are
quarantined at ingestion, and query-time traversal uses only edges whose
direction has been verified. This protects retrieval from propagating a
potentially incorrect legal relationship, although the remaining graph-quality
limitations are discussed in Section~\ref{sec:discussion}.

\subsection{QA Benchmark Dataset}
\label{sec:qa-benchmark}

The benchmark contains \textbf{500 Vietnamese legal questions}: 400 answerable
questions and 100 deliberately unanswerable questions. Questions were created
from bundles of approximately three to five related provisions and chunks using
\texttt{gemini-3.1-flash-lite}. A separate verification pass checked whether a
candidate was answerable from the supplied evidence, free of unsupported
claims, and correctly labeled by category and difficulty. Only candidates that
passed every check were retained.

The final generation run sampled 515 documents and 1,697 provisions, with a
72.41\% verifier pass rate before diversity filtering and deduplication. The
final set includes 265 single-hop, 106 citation, 96 legal-validity, 21
multi-hop, and 12 cross-document questions. Answer types span abstractive,
boolean, extractive, and unanswerable responses. The unanswerable cases are
constructed without supporting corpus evidence so that abstention behavior can
be measured separately from answer generation.

\subsection{Ground Truth Construction}
\label{sec:ground-truth}

Each query records its question, category, answer type, reference answer, and
evidence provenance:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
Q_i = \{&\mathrm{qa\_id}, \mathrm{question}, \mathrm{category},\\
&\mathrm{answer\_type}, \mathrm{reference\_answer}, \mathrm{ground\_truth}\}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

The ground-truth object identifies the supporting document, provision, and
chunk IDs. Gold chunk IDs support Recall@$k$, Hit@$k$, MRR@$k$, and nDCG@$k$
for answerable questions; unanswerable questions have empty evidence sets by
design. This provenance makes the reported retrieval results auditable, but it
does not replace expert judgment of whether an answer is legally complete or
correct.
