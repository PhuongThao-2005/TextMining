\section{Dataset and Benchmark}
\label{sec:dataset-benchmark}

This section describes the Vietnamese legal corpus, the structural design of the G-LRAG Knowledge Graph, and the 500-query legal QA benchmark dataset used to evaluate the L\_RAG system.

\subsection{Corpus Description}
\label{sec:corpus}

The legal dataset comprises a massive collection of Vietnamese legal instruments, including National Assembly Laws, Government Decrees, Prime Minister Decisions, and Ministry Circulars. Raw legal documents undergo structured parsing to preserve document metadata, hierarchical provisions, and passage chunks.

Table~\ref{tab:corpus-stats} summarizes the core scale and statistics of the processed corpus.

\begin{table*}[t]

\small
\caption{Overall Statistics of the Vietnamese Legal Corpus.}
\label{tab:corpus-stats}
\begin{tabular}{llr}
\toprule
\textbf{Entity Type} & \textbf{Description} & \textbf{Total Count} \\
\midrule
\textbf{Documents} & Full legal instruments (Laws, Decrees, etc.) & 151,624 \\
\textbf{Provisions} & Hierarchical units (Articles, Clauses, Items) & 1,386,267 \\

Each passage chunk is enriched with metadata prior to indexing:
\begin{equation}
    \begin{aligned}
        \text{Chunk\_Meta} = \{&\text{chunk\_id}, \\
        &\text{doc\_id}, \text{provision\_id}, \\
        &\text{title}, \text{issuing\_authority}, \\
        &\text{effective\_date}\}
    \end{aligned}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Knowledge Graph Structure (G-LRAG Schema)}
\label{sec:kg-schema}

The G-LRAG Knowledge Graph models statutory entities as nodes and structural
and legal dependencies as typed directed edges.

\subsubsection{Node Schema}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Document Node ($D$):} Represents a full legal instrument. Attributes: \texttt{doc\_id}, \texttt{title}, \texttt{authority\_rank}, \texttt{issuing\_body}, \texttt{effective\_date}, \texttt{validity\_status}.
    \item \textbf{Provision Node ($P$):} Represents a logical statutory section (Article/Clause). Attributes: \texttt{provision\_id}, \texttt{header}, \texttt{provision\_type}.
    \item \textbf{Chunk Node ($C$):} Represents an indexed passage. Attributes: \texttt{chunk\_id}, \texttt{text}, \texttt{sequence\_num}.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Edge Schema}
Relationships are divided into structural hierarchy and inter-document legal relationships:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Structural Hierarchy Edges:} 
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \texttt{DOCUMENT\_HAS\_PROVISION}: Connects Document $D$ to child Provision $P$.
        \item \texttt{PROVISION\_HAS\_CHUNK}: Connects Provision $P$ to child Chunk $C$.
        \item \texttt{CHUNK\_NEXT}: Sequentially links adjacent sibling chunks $C_i \rightarrow C_{i+1}$ within reading order.
        \item \texttt{PROVISION\_NEXT}: Sequentially links adjacent provisions $P_j \rightarrow P_{j+1}$.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Legal Relationship Edges:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \texttt{AMENDS}: Indicates document $D_A$ amends provision/document $D_B$.
        \item \texttt{REPLACES}: Indicates document $D_A$ replaces document $D_B$.
        \item \texttt{CITES}: Indicates document $D_A$ references document $D_B$.
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Quarantine \& Verification Integrity Gating}
To prevent graph poisoning, G-LRAG enforces two strict integrity rules:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Quarantine Gating:} Unverified or draft records are quarantined during ingestion.
    \item \textbf{Verified-Only Traversal:} Path traversals only traverse edges where \texttt{direction\_verified == True}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{QA Benchmark Dataset}
\label{sec:qa-benchmark}

To evaluate legal retrieval and answer generation, we constructed a benchmark of \textbf{500 Vietnamese legal questions} from the processed G-LRAG corpus using a grounded synthetic QA pipeline.

Questions were generated with \texttt{gemini-3.1-flash-lite} from context bundles containing approximately 3--5 related provisions and chunks. The model was instructed to formulate realistic Vietnamese legal questions using only the supplied evidence. Each candidate was subsequently evaluated in a separate verification pass for answer correctness, evidence sufficiency, hallucination avoidance, category correctness, and difficulty correctness. Only candidates passing all verification criteria were retained.

The final generation run sampled 515 documents and 1,697 provisions and achieved a verifier pass rate of 72.41\%. After diversity filtering and deduplication, the final benchmark contains \textbf{500 questions}: 265 single-hop, 106 citation, 96 legal-validity, 21 multi-hop, and 12 cross-document questions.

The dataset contains \textbf{400 answerable} and \textbf{100 unanswerable} questions. Answer types include abstractive, boolean, extractive, and unanswerable responses. Unanswerable questions are intentionally constructed without supporting corpus evidence to evaluate the system's ability to abstain from unsupported answers.

\subsection{Ground Truth Construction}
\label{sec:ground-truth}

Each benchmark query contains the question, category, answer type, reference answer, and evidence provenance:

\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
Q_i = \{&
\mathrm{qa\_id}, \mathrm{question}, \mathrm{category}, \\
&\mathrm{answer\_type}, \mathrm{reference\_answer}, \mathrm{ground\_truth}
\}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

The ground-truth object contains the supporting \texttt{document\_ids}, \texttt{provision\_ids}, and \texttt{chunk\_ids}. For answerable questions, the gold chunk identifiers are used to evaluate retrieval with Recall@$k$, Hit@$k$, MRR@$k$, and nDCG@$k$. For unanswerable questions, the ground-truth evidence sets are empty by design.
