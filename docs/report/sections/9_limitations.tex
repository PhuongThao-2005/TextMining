\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The corpus can contain missing body text, incomplete metadata, external
references, and uncertain relationship direction. The in-memory graph also has
a substantial memory footprint at the current corpus scale. More importantly,
retrieval and generation results are bounded by the benchmark and its evidence
annotations: a fluent answer, lexical overlap, or well-formed citation is not
evidence of legal correctness.

The prompt analysis is limited to saved output records. It contains no raw
chain-of-thought, reasoning-token counts, cost data, semantic claim labels, or
human assessment of citation entailment. Four DeepSeek Base/CoT cases have
different ordered top-10 chunk sequences, and the Qwen3-8B Base/CoT manifests
use different code snapshots, timeouts, and retry limits. Those model-pair
comparisons are consequently descriptive. L\_RAG is a research prototype and
must not substitute for qualified legal review.

\section*{Ethical Considerations}
\label{sec:ethics}

Legal QA may affect access to rights, benefits, obligations, and remedies.
Systems built on this work should show the cited source and validity metadata,
make abstention visible, and encourage users to verify the current legal
instrument before acting. Evaluation and deployment must also account for
uneven corpus coverage, language variation, privacy in user queries, and the
risk that a confident automated explanation receives undue trust.
