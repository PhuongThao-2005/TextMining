\section{Discussion and Conclusion}

\begin{frame}{Interpretation and Design Implications}
\small
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Metadata changes the level of success:} it improves document identification, but can push the exact gold chunk lower in the ranking.
  \item \textbf{Current hybrid retrieval is not a good default:} its paired recall loss and large latency cost outweigh the observed benefit under the tested settings.
  \item \textbf{Current graph expansion trades coverage for density:} adjacent passages can replace high-confidence seed evidence within a fixed context budget.
  \item \textbf{Reranking is the clearest positive result:} every tested cross-encoder improves Recall, MRR, and nDCG over RRF-only at a fixed candidate pool.
\end{enumerate}
\vspace{0.2cm}
\begin{block}{What can still change?}
These findings apply to the tested index, tokenization, fusion constant, candidate budgets, graph policy, and benchmark---not to every hybrid or GraphRAG design.
\end{block}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Limitations and Ethical Considerations}
\small
\begin{columns}[T]
  \column{0.49\textwidth}
    \textbf{Threats to validity}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Only 500 questions; multi-hop, hard, and cross-document slices are small.
      \item Gold chunks may omit legally sufficient alternatives.
      \item Candidate budgets differ across experiment families.
      \item One encoder, generator, prompt, and corpus version were tested.
      \item Graph relationships still contain direction and validity uncertainty.
    \end{itemize}
  \column{0.48\textwidth}
    \textbf{Responsible use}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Expose citations and validity metadata.
      \item Abstain when evidence is insufficient.
      \item Protect privacy in user questions.
      \item Account for uneven legal-corpus coverage.
      \item Require qualified human legal review.
    \end{itemize}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Conclusion and Future Work}
\small
\begin{block}{Final conclusion}
The controlled study supports \textbf{dense retrieval followed by a cross-encoder}, with \textbf{Jina} as the current quality--latency candidate. Metadata helps at document level; the tested hybrid and graph policies require redesign before becoming defaults.
\end{block}
\vspace{0.2cm}
\textbf{Next experiments}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Collect blinded labels for legal correctness, completeness, faithfulness, and citation quality.
  \item Sweep candidate-pool size, RRF parameters, and graph-context budgets.
  \item Implement seed-preserving graph expansion and validate edge directions.
  \item Evaluate generator reasoning strategies and larger, more diverse benchmarks.
  \item Measure full-online p95 latency, GPU memory, and throughput.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{0.15cm}
\centering
\hi{The results are benchmark-bounded engineering evidence---not certification of legal reliability.}
\end{frame}
