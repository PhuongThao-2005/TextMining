\section{Discussion and Conclusion}

\begin{frame}{Discussion: Key Findings}
\scriptsize
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Metadata:} improves document identification (+4.5 pp) but lowers exact-chunk MRR (-5.9 pp).
  \item \textbf{Hybrid and graph retrieval:} both reduce exact evidence recall under the tested budgets; hybrid adds substantial latency, while graph expansion improves identity precision.
  \item \textbf{Reranking:} every tested cross-encoder improves retrieval quality over RRF-only. Jina leads retrieval metrics, BGE leads lexical overlap, and mMiniLM is fastest.
  \item \textbf{Prompting across models:} across all four LLMs, CoT does not improve EM, F1, or ROUGE-L. It increases latency and lowers structural coverage; decision and citation changes are small and mixed. Only GPT-4o-mini has fully matched contexts, so cross-model comparisons are descriptive.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{0.12cm}
\begin{block}{Scope}
Conclusions are limited to the tested corpus, index, budgets, graph policy, and 500-query benchmark.
\end{block}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Limitations and Responsible Use}
\small
\begin{columns}[T]
  \column{0.49\textwidth}
    \textbf{Validity limits}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item 500 questions; difficult slices are small and gold evidence may omit valid alternatives.
      \item Results depend on one corpus/encoder and unequal candidate budgets.
      \item Graph validity, semantic correctness, reasoning traces, and cost remain incompletely measured.
    \end{itemize}
  \column{0.48\textwidth}
    \textbf{Responsible use}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Show citations and validity metadata; abstain when evidence is weak.
      \item Protect privacy and acknowledge incomplete corpus coverage.
      \item Require qualified legal review; this is decision support, not legal advice.
    \end{itemize}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Conclusion and Next Steps}
\scriptsize
\begin{block}{Bottom line}
The strongest tested configuration is \textbf{dense retrieval + cross-encoder reranking}, with \textbf{Jina} offering the best quality--latency balance. Metadata helps at document level, but the current hybrid and graph policies need redesign. Across four LLMs, CoT adds cost without improving lexical answer quality.
\end{block}
\vspace{0.2cm}
\textbf{Next steps}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Add blinded labels for correctness, faithfulness, completeness, and citation quality.
  \item Tune candidate pools, RRF, graph budgets, and seed-preserving expansion.
  \item Align provenance and rerun incomplete model comparisons.
  \item Test broader Vietnamese benchmarks; measure end-to-end p95, memory, throughput, and cost.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{0.15cm}
\centering
\hi{The results are benchmark-bounded engineering evidence---not certification of legal reliability.}
\end{frame}
